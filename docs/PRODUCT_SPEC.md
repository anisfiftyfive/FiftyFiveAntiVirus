# Product spec

**Product:** DroidSentinel  
**Users:** Everyday Android users, not SOC analysts  
**Job:** See what is on the phone, understand what deserves attention, decide what to uninstall or revoke.

## In scope

- Installed-app inventory and permission risk
- User-selected APK hashing and Hypatia signature matching
- Local history
- Honest scanner/database status
- Optional on-device explanations

## Out of scope

- Real-time filesystem monitoring
- Automatic uninstall or quarantine
- Cloud reputation lookups
- Guaranteed malware detection
- Enterprise device management

## Success for the hackathon

A polished APK, real scans, real statuses, tests around scanner honesty, and an auditable AI development trail.
